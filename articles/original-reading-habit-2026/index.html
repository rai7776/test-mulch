How to Make English Reading a Daily Habit

1日10分の英語多読習慣を無理なく続けるための実践ガイド。読む時間、教材の選び方、復習方法を学びます。

単語登録・読み上げ・しおりなどを使えます。本文はこのページでも全文読めます。

Many people decide to read more English at the beginning of a new month. They choose a long novel, buy a new notebook, and make an ambitious plan. For the first few days, everything goes well. Then school becomes busy, work takes longer than expected, or another activity seems more interesting. The reading plan quietly disappears.

A better plan starts with a smaller goal. Instead of promising to read for one hour, choose ten minutes. Ten minutes is long enough to enjoy a short article, but short enough to fit into an ordinary day. When the goal feels manageable, it becomes easier to begin.

Next, connect reading to an activity you already do. You might read after breakfast, while waiting for a train, or before turning off the light at night. The time does not need to be perfect. What matters is giving your reading habit a familiar place in the day.

Choose material that is interesting to you. Someone who loves sports may enjoy a story about a famous match. Someone who likes cooking may prefer a recipe or an interview with a chef. Difficult reading is not always better reading. If every sentence contains several unknown words, even a short text can feel exhausting.

While you read, avoid stopping for every unfamiliar word. First, try to understand the main idea of the paragraph. Mark words that seem important, and return to them after you finish. Learning three useful expressions carefully may be more valuable than collecting twenty words you never review.

After reading, spend one minute thinking about what you understood. Can you explain the article in two simple English sentences? Can you tell a friend one new thing you learned? This small review turns reading into an active learning experience.

Some days you will have more time, and some days you will have less. On a busy day, a single paragraph is enough to keep the habit alive. Missing one day does not mean the plan has failed. Simply begin again the next day.

A reading habit is not built by one perfect study session. It grows through many ordinary sessions. Start with a small goal, choose texts you enjoy, and make it easy to return tomorrow.

表現と文法のポイント

Instead of + -ing

「〜する代わりに」。instead of promising to read for one hour は「1時間読むと約束する代わりに」という意味です。

Instead of reading for an hour, read for ten minutes.

What matters is ...

重要な点を強調する表現。「大切なのは〜ということだ」と説明するときに使えます。

What matters is giving your habit a familiar place in the day.

avoid + -ing

avoid の後ろには動名詞を置いて「〜するのを避ける」と表します。

Avoid stopping for every unfamiliar word.

読解チェック

問題の答えを考えてから「答えと解説」を開いてください。

1. Why does the article recommend a ten-minute reading goal?

答えと解説を見る
Because it is easier to fit into an ordinary day and easier to begin.

本文第2段落では、小さな目標の方が管理しやすく、始めやすいと説明しています。

2. What should you try to understand before checking every new word?

答えと解説を見る
The main idea of the paragraph.

未知語をすべて調べる前に段落全体の主旨をつかむことをすすめています。

3. What should you do after missing a day?

答えと解説を見る
Begin again the next day.

1日休んでも失敗ではありません。翌日再開すればよいというメッセージです。
