The Notebook on the Last Train

終電で見つけた英語学習ノートをきっかけに、人とつながる理由を描くオリジナル短編です。

単語登録・読み上げ・しおりなどを使えます。本文はこのページでも全文読めます。

It was nearly eleven at night when Hana stepped onto the last train. The station was quiet, and most of the seats were empty. She had spent the evening helping her friend prepare for a job interview. Now she wanted nothing more than to go home and sleep.

Across from her sat an older man wearing a blue coat. He was looking through a small notebook and quietly repeating a few English phrases. When the train stopped, he stood up quickly and hurried toward the door. Just before the doors closed, Hana noticed the notebook on his seat.

“Excuse me!” she called, but the man was already on the platform. The doors closed, and the train began to move. Hana picked up the notebook. On the first page, someone had written a name and a simple sentence: “I am learning English because I want to speak with my granddaughter.”

Hana looked at the next few pages. They were filled with short conversations about families, food, and travel. Some sentences had been crossed out and corrected. A photograph of a smiling little girl was tucked between two pages.

At the next station, Hana went to the information desk. A tired station employee listened to her story. “We can keep it here,” he said. “If the owner asks for it, we will contact you.” Hana wrote down her phone number and went home.

The next afternoon, her phone rang. The station employee told her that the notebook's owner had returned. He wanted to thank her in person. Hana agreed to meet him at the station after work.

When they met, the man looked relieved. “This notebook is very important to me,” he explained. “My granddaughter lives overseas. She is visiting next month, and I want to surprise her by speaking English.”

Hana smiled. “Then you should practice with someone. I can help for a few minutes if you like.” The man opened his notebook and pointed at a sentence. “How was your flight?” he read carefully. Hana answered as if she were his granddaughter, and they both laughed.

Before leaving, he said, “I thought I had lost weeks of work. But perhaps I found a new friend instead.”

On her train home, Hana remembered the sentence on the notebook's first page. She had never thought much about why people study languages. That night, she realized that the best reason could be very simple: there was someone you wanted to talk to.

表現と文法のポイント

nothing more than ...

「ただ〜だけを望む」という気持ちを表す言い方です。

She wanted nothing more than to go home and sleep.

as if + 文

「まるで〜であるかのように」と、実際とは異なる状況を想像して表現します。

Hana answered as if she were his granddaughter.

instead

文末の instead は「その代わりに」。予想外の良い展開や選択の変更を表すときにも使えます。

Perhaps I found a new friend instead.

読解チェック

問題の答えを考えてから「答えと解説」を開いてください。

1. Why was the older man studying English?

答えと解説を見る
He wanted to speak English with his granddaughter.

ノートの最初のページと男性の説明から理由が分かります。

2. What did Hana do with the notebook?

答えと解説を見る
She took it to the station's information desk.

次の駅で駅員に預けました。

3. What did the older man mean by finding a new friend?

答えと解説を見る
Meeting Hana gave him a new person to practice English with.

ノートを落としたことが新しい出会いにつながった、という結末です。
